  038 Warranty Services Checklist 
Overview: 
1.  Confirm the product is from UFP Industries, Inc. 
2.  Obtain a completed Case Information Form for the product brand  from the consumer, selling store or distributor - 
required for review. 
3.  Obtain a copy of all relevant material and labor invoices – required for review 
4.  Obtain photos that clearly convey the issue of the impacted product(s) – required for review  
a.  For railings, a photo of the entire rail section e.g. post-to-post and from the deck surface to just above the 
post caps. 
b.  In focus (close-up) photo(s) of the problem(s) with the rail system 
c.  For decking, a photo looking down at a 3’ to 4’ square area 
d.  In focus (close-up) photo(s) showing the concern(s) with the decking 
e.  In all cases, include at least two (2) photos, taken from out in the yard, showing the entire deck from the 
ground up. 
f.  For decking, request End Tag(s) and/or photos of the code printed on the boards for follow up to obtain 
manufacturing information from the plant’s production database. 
5.  If appropriate, after review of documentation above, provide the consumer a copy of the product installation 
instructions and relevant warranty or refer the consumer to the website for that information based on brand of 
product:  
 
www.verandadeck.ca 
www.deckorators.com  
6.  If the consumer’s concern does not qualify for compensation or replacement provided by the warranty, explain that 
to the consumer. Do not imply that it may qualify when it does not. 
7.  Keep your “filter” on! Provide simple answers and do not elaborate to impress. Less is more! 
Warranty Services Notes:  
A.  A claim can only be thoroughly reviewed and processed once the required information noted above in #’s 2, 3, & 4 
has been received. 
B.  Limit total file size of e-mail attachments to 5MB or less to ensure inbound servers do not filter the message. Forward 
the information via one or multiple e-mails sent on the same date.  
C.  To facilitate e-mail communications, enter the consumer name, claim number, and selling store at the beginning of 
the message subject line. Example: “ Re: Doe 334-1234 Macy’s… ” 
D.  E-mail the required information to both: 
1.  Your UFP Industries Account Manager: ______________________________________________ 
2.  WarrantyUCP@ufpi.com  
E.  Additional photos and/or information may be needed in order to properly evaluate a claim. 
F.  An onsite inspection of a project may be required if the information submitted is vague or the nature of the claim 
indicates an inspection is necessary. Note: Always have a representative from the selling dealer or distributor present 
with you during an onsite inspection. Extra eyes & ears, and a witness to what was discussed. Going alone is highly 
discouraged. 
G.  Qualifying warranty claims will be resolved in a timely manner. 
H.  A fully executed Settlement Agreement & Release, prepared by Universal Legal, signed by the consumer(s) may be 
required before the resolution steps are completed.
