Installation
Shadow Line+ can be installed either horizontally or
vertically, depending on the building design. It is installed in
a similar way to tongue and groove wood siding, with a
fastener through the tongue. The groove of the next board
then covers the head of the fastener. The fasteners need to
external grade and be the correct size for code compliance.
We recommend the use of a drainable housewrap to be
used in between sheathing and siding, to help allow the
free flow of moisture behind the boards. Alternatively, furring
strips can be used to provide the gap behind the boards.
