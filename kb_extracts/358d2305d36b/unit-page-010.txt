Fencing Grades
Fencing can be S1S2E or S4S boards in widths from 1x4 thru 1x12 
or shorter lengths of decking like 5/4x6x6 or 8'. Fencing can also be 
shorter lengths of pattern stock like T&G V4E Fineline, Shiplap or 
Channel. Any length can be used in fencing from 3' to longer, from 
vertical or horizontal applications, let your imagination decide.
