PANELING
Western Red Cedar Kiln-dried Western Red Cedar paneling from Russin is available in both clear and  
knotty grades. Paneling creates beautiful exterior siding, soffits, interior walls, and  
ceilings. Products may be installed vertically, horizontally or diagonally, with each  
method providing a different design style.
