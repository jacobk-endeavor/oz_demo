Russin's Western Red Cedar product  
offering provides quality builders, architects 
and homeowners with an amazing array of 
choices for a wide variety of exterior and  
interior applications including siding, trim, 
paneling, decking, and so much more. 
Western Red Cedar is well-known for its  
natural beauty and durability, resistance  
to rot and decay, dimensional stability,  
and ability to easily accept a wide variety  
of finishes with excellent results - from  
oils and stains, to solid primers and paint. 
 
Western Red Cedar is lightweight,  
making it easy to handle. Its texture  
makes it easy to cut, rout, saw and  
nail with standard tools. For your next  
project specify Western Red Cedar, one  
of the world’s finest building materials.
