Bevel Siding
Russin distributes a full assortment of kiln-dried Western Red Cedar  
bevel siding in both clear and knotty grades. Bevel is the most common  
typeof siding and provides an attractive shadow line which varies with  
the thickness of the chosen siding. 
Primed Clear Fingerjoint
Primed fingerjoint bevel siding by Russin is an ideal  
cost-effective alternative when a painted surface and  
long lengths are desired. Primed fingerjoint bevel  
siding provides building contractors and homeowners  
with the warmth, beauty and natural decay-resistance  
of real Western Red Cedar, plus the built-in protection  
of a superior quality primer. Depending upon the size,  
one or two coats of primer in Cape Cod Gray is  
machine-applied at our state-of-the-art facility. 
Bevel siding is all 16' lengths with a maximum  
of 10% trimbacks.
